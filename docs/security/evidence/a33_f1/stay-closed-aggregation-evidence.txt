2. Exact model semantics

Only the self-contained integration-test model changes:
  crates/qbind-consensus/tests/a33_f1_stay_closed_quarantine_model_tests.rs
SHA-256: cf7631950e1bb611f1450f73ff67ce64b5397437cd830becce7da2594c1706a3

Each worker retains a sticky known_effect bit, sticky conflict bit, its
highest observed signing stage, enqueue observation, completion observation
and separate peer-delivery observation. Known reservation, key invocation,
signature, enqueue, delivery and generic worker effects cannot be reset by
completion, repeated refresh, cancellation or late observations.

Completion/delivery observations start Unknown. Unknown can become NoEffect
or EffectObserved; exact duplicates are harmless. A different observation
after a resolved observation, including a later Unknown, is sticky Conflict.
Conflict cannot become resolved through subsequent updates.

Signing stages progress in fixture order from request through reservation,
key invocation and signature. Exact repeated stages are harmless. Backward
stages preserve the highest observed stage and mark conflict. A changed
stage after terminal completion also conflicts: this model has no trusted
ordering evidence to resolve the late update. A NoEffect completion after
a known worker effect conflicts regardless of arrival order. These are
conservative model rules, not a newly approved production event protocol.

Enqueue is a known effect, NOT peer delivery. First enqueue after terminal
completion conflicts; repeated enqueue does not erase the first effect.
Worker completion and delivery remain separate: completed enqueue can
consistently coexist with NoEffect delivery, but enqueue cannot support a
claim that the worker itself had NoEffect. Contradictory delivery updates
conflict independently.

Domain summaries inspect ALL relevant workers:
  Conflict: at least one conflicting worker, with per-worker known_effect
    and highest stage/enqueue still retained. Conflict does not mean that
    observed effects disappeared or that their absence is established.
  EffectUnresolved: known effect AND unresolved completion/delivery,
    including a separate unknown worker in either worker order.
  EffectObserved: known effect, all required observations resolved.
  Unknown: unresolved observations without any known effect.
  NoEffect: all required observations consistently resolve without effects
    (or no workers in that domain).
Only consistent NoEffect/EffectObserved completion can satisfy a worker
barrier; outbound also requires resolved delivery. Conflict immediately
latches quarantine. Late updates never reopen admission or clear a latch.
Effects from snapshot/epoch/callback workers, signing, outbound and storage
workers use the same aggregation rule.

Related non-worker summaries also retain known effects: an accepted engine
prefix plus uncertain current item becomes EffectUnresolved, not Unknown;
an acknowledged intent plus uncertain outcome remains EffectUnresolved;
publication visibly present despite an intent error is EffectUnresolved.
No intent acknowledgement or successful completion is inferred from visible
publication. The intent gate, engine prefix/report/map and storage outcome
remain separate; no rollback or response-level atomicity is claimed.
